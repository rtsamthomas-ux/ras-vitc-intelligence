# VIT Chennai IEEE Student Branch website

## About the student branch
The VIT Chennai IEEE student branch site says the branch strives for excellence and innovation and aims to advance humanity through technology. It says it organises events, workshops, hackathons and project labs, and that its members have won competitions at national and international level (this is the branch's own claim).

## Robotics and Automation Society
The same site repeats the society's aim: the IEEE Robotics and Automation Society's objectives are scientific, literary and educational in character.
