# IEEE VIT Chennai - Societies

## Societies listed
The IEEE VIT Chennai website lists the societies its student branch is associated with. These include the IEEE Robotics and Automation Society, the IEEE Computer Society, the IEEE Signal Processing Society and IEEE Women in Engineering.

## Robotics and Automation Society description
The page describes the IEEE Robotics and Automation Society as dedicated to promoting scientific, educational and literary endeavours. Its mission, as stated there, is to advance the theory, practice and scientific foundations of robotics and automation and related fields, while upholding high professional standards in line with the IEEE Constitution.
