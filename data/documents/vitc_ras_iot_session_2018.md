# IEEE RAS VIT Chennai - chapter activities report (Chapter Initiative Grant attachment)

## IoT in Robotics demo session (19 December 2018)
The IEEE RAS VIT Chennai Student Chapter held an interactive demo session titled "IoT in Robotics" as part of its regular activities. It took place on 19 December 2018 at the VIT Chennai campus. The session was handled by an industry expert, Ms. Vasanthi from SANDS India Private Ltd. The faculty coordinator, Dr. Suchetha M, opened it with a short introduction to IEEE and IEEE RAS. The session combined two technology megatrends, IoT and robotics, which the report describes as important areas of interest for researchers worldwide. Students interested in pursuing robotics and automation attended, asked questions at the end, and the resource person was felicitated by the faculty coordinators.

## Chapter inauguration
The report also describes the inauguration of the chapter, which it calls one of its kind among IEEE societies in the Madras Section. The faculty coordinator, Dr. Suchetha M (Associate Professor, School of Electronics Engineering), gave the welcome address. The chief guest was honoured, the society's vice president at the time gave a short talk on what IEEE is and aims to achieve, the core committee members were honoured, and a video presentation explained what IEEE RAS VIT Chennai aims to achieve.
