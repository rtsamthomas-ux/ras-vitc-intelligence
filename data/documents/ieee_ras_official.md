# IEEE Robotics and Automation Society - official website

## What IEEE RAS is
The IEEE Robotics and Automation Society (IEEE RAS) presents itself on its official website as the leading organization in research and technological developments in robotics and automation worldwide. It is a society within IEEE.

## What the society offers
The official site is organised around these areas: About and governance, Members (including technical committees and programs such as the Distinguished Lecturers Program), Chapters, groups for Students, Industry Professionals, Young Professionals and Women in Engineering, Conferences, Publications, Education, Awards and Industry activities.

## Conferences and publications
RAS conferences listed on the site include ICRA, CASE and IROS. RAS publications include the IEEE Transactions on Robotics (T-RO), Automation Science and Engineering (T-ASE), Haptics, Field Robotics, Medical Robotics and Bionics, Robot Learning and Soft Robotics, plus Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L), the RA Magazine and RA Practice.

## Education and students
The Education section includes RAS University, competitions and a Technical Education Program. The site says RAS University offers free education. There is a dedicated Students page with resources, programs and opportunities for students interested in robotics.

## Local chapters
The site invites people to join a RAS Chapter to connect with students and professionals on campus and in the region. Benefits listed include joining local events hosted by regional and sectional chapters and receiving the RAS newsletter. It also mentions applying to EPICS (Engineering Projects in Community Service).
