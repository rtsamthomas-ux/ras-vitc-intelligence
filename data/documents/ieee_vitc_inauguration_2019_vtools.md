# IEEE VIT Chennai - Inauguration of IEEE Activities (24 July 2019), IEEE vTools event record

## The event
The IEEE Student Branch of VIT Chennai held an "Inauguration of IEEE Activities" on 24 July 2019 at the VIT Chennai campus. According to the official IEEE vTools event record, it was an orientation for new undergraduate students that introduced the IEEE societies at VIT Chennai and encouraged students to become members.

## Societies that took part
The societies introduced were the Power Electronics Society, Women in Engineering, the Robotics and Automation Society (RAS), the Signal Processing Society and the Computer Society. The chief guest was Prasanth Mohan, described as the founding chair of the IEEEXtreme public relations committee. The student branch counsellor was Dr. Vydeki D.

## The Robotics and Automation Society's part
The record says the faculty coordinator and the student coordinator of the Robotics and Automation Society, Prof. Suchetha and Siva Shankar, briefed the new students about the society's projects and activities. These were the RAS coordinators in 2019; the record says nothing about who holds these roles now.
