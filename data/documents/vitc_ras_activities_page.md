# VIT Chennai (SENSE) - IEEE RAS Student Chapter Activities

## Where the official activity reports are
The School of Electronics Engineering (SENSE) section of the VIT Chennai website has a page titled "IEEE Robotics And Automation Society (RAS) Student Chapter Activities". It links to two activity reports: "2020-21 Activities" and "2019-20 Activities". The event details are in those linked reports rather than on the page itself.

## Where the chapter sits
The page is hosted under the School of Electronics Engineering (SENSE) at VIT Chennai, Vandalur-Kelambakkam Road, Chennai.
