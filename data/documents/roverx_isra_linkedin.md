# IEEE RAS Student Chapter VIT Chennai - LinkedIn posts

## RoverX - The Hardware Workshop 2025
RoverX - The Hardware Workshop 2025 was organised by the IEEE Robotics and Automation Society (VITC Student Chapter) as part of TechnoVIT, VIT Chennai's national-level technology festival held 31 October to 2 November 2025. It is a hands-on hardware workshop where students build DIY rovers and robotic systems using the ESP32 microcontroller, aiming to encourage creativity, innovation and real-world problem solving. The post was a call for sponsors and industry partners working in robotics, automation, AI, AGVs, AMRs or humanoid systems.
