# IEEE RAS VIT Chennai - recruitment website (ieeerasvitc.tech)

## What the site is
ieeerasvitc.tech is the recruitment website of the IEEE Robotics and Automation Society (IEEE RAS) student chapter at VIT Chennai. Its public landing page is headed "IEEE RAS - Robotics & Automation, Recruitment 2026-27, VIT / Chennai" with the slogan "Build the future. Join IEEE RAS".

## Recruitment 2026-27
When checked on 20 September 2026, the landing page said that applications for Recruitment 2026-27 were open, that the chapter is recruiting into 4 departments, and that intake is limited. The "Join IEEE RAS" button leads to a dashboard that needs a login. The sign-in and sign-up screens ask students to use their @vitstudent.ac.in email address, so applying is meant for VIT students.

## What the public page does not say
The public landing page does not list the names of the 4 departments, the application deadline, the selection rounds or the tasks. Those details are only visible after logging in, so this assistant cannot verify them.
