# RAScade - Devfolio page

## Overview
RAScade's Devfolio page describes it as a prestigious international technical hackathon that invites students to collaborate, tackle real-world challenges and build solutions alongside a diverse pool of global participants, competing for awards. Its tagline is "Innovate, Iterate, Inspire", and it links to rascade.in.

## Dates and schedule (as shown on 20 September 2026)
The page shows the event in Chennai, India. Its schedule (India time) lists: registrations open 1 July 2024, registrations close 31 October 2024, and the hackathon on Friday 8 November 2024, starting 8:00 AM and ending 6:00 PM. The page now gives no duration in hours. An earlier version of this page, reviewed before 20 September 2026, gave 27-29 September 2024 and described a 48-hour event, so the dates appear to have been changed on the page.

## Tracks, teams and prizes
Tracks: Hardware, DEV Tool, Healthcare, Social Inclusivity, Web3 and Open Innovation. Teams have 2 to 4 members and registration is free. The listed prize pool is USD 550 (Aptos USD 250, Polygon USD 200, ETHIndia USD 100). Sponsors shown include Polygon, ETHIndia, Aptos, QuillAudits, AoPS Academy, Altium, blockflow.network, Flatlogic, Beeceptor, Certopus, InterviewBuddy and .xyz.
