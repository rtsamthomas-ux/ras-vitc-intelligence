# HackVerse: Into the Web - IEEE RAS VIT Chennai hackathon (Devfolio page)

## Overview
HackVerse: Into the Web is a Spider-Man-themed, story-driven hackathon organised by the IEEE Robotics and Automation Society (IEEE RAS) VIT Chennai Student Chapter as part of TechnoVIT 2026. The Devfolio page lists it as running on 1-2 September 2026 in Chennai, India. When checked on 20 September 2026, the page said applications were closed and the hackathon had ended.

## Format
Teams have 3 to 5 members and registration is free. The page describes the event as a 24-hour journey through three progressively harder sprints, framed as a hero's journey:
- Web-Slinger Sprint: a fast "vibe-coding" round where teams turn an idea into a prototype using modern frameworks and AI tools.
- Spider-Sense Sprint: a short competitive-coding round on algorithms, logic and data structures (DSA), judged on speed and accuracy.
- Multiverse Sprint: teams take their idea beyond a prototype and build a working, deployment-ready product.
The page's pitch is to take an idea, build it, deploy it and prove it. Participants must follow the Code of Conduct.

## What teams can build
There is no fixed problem statement, domain or tech stack. Examples the page lists include an AI agent, a robotics or IoT system, a fintech platform, a healthcare solution, cybersecurity, Web3 and automation tools.

## Prizes
The page lists a total prize pool of USD 3,100, plus special prizes for the hardware track (Spider-Man masks and goodies for the best innovation). The chapter's Instagram handle given on the page is @ieeerasvitc.
